Our story

A house of colour, culture and personal guidance.

Sahney Pagri House brings the richness of Punjabi tradition into a clear, thoughtful and welcoming fabric experience.

What defines us

Tradition deserves attention to detail.

Choosing a pagri is personal. The shade, fabric fall and tying style should feel right for the person wearing it—not simply look good on a screen. That is why the Sahney experience is built around listening first and narrowing the right options with you.

From everyday Sikh pagri fabrics to wedding safas, kalgis, sehras and traditional clothing materials, every enquiry begins with the occasion and the look you want to create.
