About Us

Respect for tradition. Care in every colour and detail.

SAHNEY PAGRI HOUSE helps customers explore turban fabrics, Punjabi clothing fabrics and groom accessories with warm personal guidance and modern convenience.

Our Story

A modern fabric experience rooted in Punjabi identity

SAHNEY PAGRI HOUSE is presented as a welcoming destination for customers who care about the meaning, colour and finish of a traditional look. The focus is not only on a single product—it is on helping each person understand how fabric, pagri shade and accessories work together.

From daily Sikh pagri fabrics to wedding-special turbans, safas, kalgis, sehras and clothing materials, the goal is to make selection clearer and more personal without inventing unsupported history or claims.

Our Customer Approach

Listen first, then narrow the right options

A useful enquiry starts with a few practical details: the occasion, outfit photo, preferred colour family, event date and quantity. This helps the shop guide customers toward relevant fabrics or accessories instead of presenting an overwhelming catalogue.

Screen colours are not exact, so final shade matching is best confirmed with the physical outfit or fabric in natural light.
